## Real-World Scenario

A technology company is developing a new electronic product as part of a confidential project. One of the company's employees



 takes a photograph of an early prototype of the product. A few days later, the photograph appears on a public online platform without the company's authorization.

The company suspects that the image may have been shared without permission and decides to investigate its origin. A digital forensics team is assigned to examine the original photograph using **ExifTool**. The investigation focuses on extracting and analyzing available metadata, including the camera make and model, date and time, GPS coordinates, and any software used to edit the image.

The objective is to uncover useful clues about where and when the photograph was taken and determine whether the metadata can help support the investigation.



 #### ZF Tool (Zaytoonah Forensic Tool)
 a lightweight, client-side digital forensics utility designed for privacy analysis and metadata inspection.

As a secure client-side web application developed for digital investigations, ZF Tool processes everything locally inside the browser with zero server-side uploads. Powered by the reliable ExifReader JavaScript library, the tool enables investigators and security enthusiasts to seamlessly extract EXIF data, timestamps, device profiles, and embedded raw GPS coordinates directly from images with complete data privacy.
